A professional-grade music studio built as a complete environment, featuring both a live recording room and a control room connected by a glass window. The space is filled with detailed, recognizable equipment and designed to feel like a real working studio.

<FullWidthImage src="/projects/music-studio/01.webp" alt="Recording room with instruments" />

The recording room houses a full drum kit, guitar amplifiers, a condenser microphone on a boom stand, and acoustic diffuser panels made from individually placed wood blocks. A neon rock hand sign adds character above the door.

<FullWidthImage src="/projects/music-studio/02.webp" alt="Control room overview" />

The control room features a mixing desk with dual monitors running a DAW, studio monitors on stands, a keyboard controller, and outboard rack gear. A leather sofa and Persian rug soften the space and make it inviting for long sessions.
