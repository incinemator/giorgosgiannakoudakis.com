A digital recreation of one of the four lions from the Morosini Fountain, a 17th-century Venetian landmark in the heart of Heraklion, Crete. The goal was to faithfully reproduce the sculptural style of the original while pushing the detail level achievable in a digital medium.

## Reference

Photographs of the actual fountain served as the primary reference, capturing the proportions, the stylized mane curls, and the weathered stone surface that centuries of exposure have produced.

## Sculpt

The sculpt focuses on the ornamental quality of the original: the rhythmic, almost decorative curls of the mane, the stylized facial features, and the powerful seated posture. A warm sandstone material was applied to echo the look of the real limestone.
