A complete interior environment built from the ground up, recreating the atmosphere of a late-1980s home office. Every object in the scene was individually modeled and textured to capture the era's distinct aesthetic.

<FullWidthImage src="/projects/80s-home-office/01.webp" alt="80s Home Office - Wide shot" />

The desk area is the focal point, cluttered with period-accurate props: an early personal computer with a green phosphor CRT, floppy disks, computing magazines, a rotary phone, and scattered paperwork. The warm desk lamp creates a focused pool of light that draws the eye.

<FullWidthImage src="/projects/80s-home-office/02.webp" alt="80s Home Office - Desk close-up" />

The living area features a full bookshelf stocked with vintage electronics, a boombox, nixie tube clock, VHS tapes, and a curated collection of books. A cozy sofa and fish tank complete the lived-in feel.

  <img src="/projects/80s-home-office/03.webp" alt="Bookshelf and sofa" class="rounded-lg" />
  <img src="/projects/80s-home-office/04.webp" alt="Bookshelf detail" class="rounded-lg" />
</ImageGrid>

Pop culture references are woven throughout the scene, including a Blade Runner poster and a second CRT television, grounding the space in its time period and giving it personality.
