An anatomical visualization of the human respiratory system, showing the right lung alongside the exposed trachea and bronchial tree. The project was built for medical illustration purposes, balancing scientific accuracy with visual clarity.

<FullWidthImage src="/projects/respiratory-system/01.jpg" alt="Lung with exposed bronchial tree" />

The lung surface features a porous, spongy texture with subtle color variation to convey the organic quality of real tissue. The bronchial branches were modeled to follow anatomically correct bifurcation patterns.
