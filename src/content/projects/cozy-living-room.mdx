This scene captures the warmth and calm of a Mediterranean morning. Sunlight pours through tall French windows, casting long shadows across patterned terracotta tiles. A balcony with classical balusters frames a hazy coastal view, while an abstract painting on the wall adds a splash of color to the sun-drenched space.

<FullWidthImage src="/projects/cozy-living-room/01.webp" alt="Living room wide shot with morning light" />

The furniture was carefully chosen to evoke a relaxed, timeless feel: handwoven rattan chairs, a matching side table, and a wooden magazine rack. Every prop, from the breakfast tray to the potted plants, was modeled to reinforce the sense of a quiet morning ritual.
