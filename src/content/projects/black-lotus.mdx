Inspired by the legendary Black Lotus card, this project explores the contrast between darkness and light through a mystical flower with bioluminescent stamens. The deep crimson petals absorb the surrounding light while the glowing core radiates energy outward.

<FullWidthImage src="/projects/black-lotus/closeup-1.jpg" alt="Black lotus close-up with glowing stamens" />

A macro view into the heart of the flower reveals the intricate stamen geometry and the neon emission shader that gives the lotus its otherworldly glow. The petal textures were crafted to mimic real organic surfaces with fine veins and translucency.

<FullWidthImage src="/projects/black-lotus/closeup-2.jpg" alt="Stamen detail with neon glow" />

From above, the flower's layered petal structure becomes visible, surrounded by detailed lily pads floating on the water's surface. The color shifts from deep purple at the outer petals to vibrant pink at the center.

<FullWidthImage src="/projects/black-lotus/top.jpg" alt="Top view on lily pads" />

The full scene places three lotuses in a pond with sun reflections dancing across the water. The composition balances the warm backlight with the cool water tones, creating a serene yet magical atmosphere.

<FullWidthImage src="/projects/black-lotus/general.jpg" alt="Three lotuses on water with sun reflection" />
