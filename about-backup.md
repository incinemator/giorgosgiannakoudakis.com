## Bio Text

With over **nine years of experience** in **3D visualization** using **Blender**, I've built a strong foundation in **modeling**, **materials**, **lighting**, and **rendering**, as well as translating real-world objects and data into compelling digital imagery. I'm comfortable learning new tools quickly and enjoy experimenting with lighting, motion, and visual storytelling.

I began working with Blender in its earlier versions, developing a strong comfort with complex interfaces and a self-driven approach to learning new tools. My early projects involved designing **promotional posters** for a radio show I hosted, transforming symbolic elements into realistic visual compositions to create a distinctive and memorable identity.

As a **3D Visualization and Software Engineer at IMAI-MedTech** in Zurich, I designed from scratch a **dedicated user interface within Blender** to convert medical images into 3D visualizations. I applied advanced material and shading techniques to enhance the visualization of critical features, processed large structured datasets, and produced animations used in funding presentations, combining software development, data processing, and visual design.

Currently, as **3D Graphics Designer at ROBO**, I create a series of **videos** for their 2026 **promotional campaign**. I design and animate the assembly of a custom Arduino-based PCB kit, building a fully equipped electronics lab in Blender with highly detailed models of real-world equipment. **High-quality texturing** is applied down to the tiniest electronic component to achieve photo-realistic effect.

Beyond Blender, I work with **Unreal Engine** to build **interactive environments** and **real-time visualization workflows**, spanning VR/AR/XR applications, asset optimization, PBR material creation, retopology, rigging, and performance optimization. I thrive at the intersection of artistic quality and technical precision.

I'm motivated by opportunities to grow through feedback and collaboration. On a personal note, bikes, sports, and movement are a genuine part of my everyday life. I ride daily and swim three to four times a week, including years of winter sea swimming in Greece.
